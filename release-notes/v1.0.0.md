EUC 1.0.0 makes conversions far more accurate on real shop pages and the extension lighter on every page.

## More prices and measurements, read correctly

- **The page's own currency.** Shared signs such as $, ¥, kr, R, ريال and درهم follow the page's currency, taken from its structured data, country domain, store path or language: `$49.990` on falabella.com is Chilean pesos, and `¥` on tmall.com is yuan.
- **New price formats:** 원, 円, 元 and ￥; CJK amounts such as `1万5000円`, `5만원` and `2억 5천만원`; ranges whose bare bound shares the other bound's currency (`1 500 - 2 700 zł`); Nordic and Czech `2 990,-` prices; three-decimal dinars (`12.900 KD`); Persian magnitude words; negative differences (`−79.01€`); and cents drawn as raised numbers (`$399⁹⁹`).
- **Prices split across elements or marked by an icon:** IKEA and Amazon split prices; toman icons on Iranian shops; currency labels such as extra.com's SAR.
- **Units:** volume (ml, L, fl oz, gal); ranges (`30 to 49 in`, `80吋至89吋`); fractions; feet and inches; W/L/H/D labels; units written straight into Chinese, Japanese or Korean text; and the word for inch in more than 20 languages.
- **Fewer false readings:** "#487 in Fashion", model names such as "Canon EOS R5", "MK02" and "Redmi 15C", and screen-reader copies of prices are no longer converted.

## Badges that stay readable

- A badge that a fixed-size box would cut off now moves just outside that box. Where nothing nearby can show it, as in Amazon colour swatches, the conversion appears on hover instead.
- Content revealed later (tabs, variant prices) and content inserted in bulk is now scanned. A page that kept rescanning itself (amazon.ae product pages) no longer does.

## Faster

- On the benchmark pages, the extension spends less than half the main-thread time of 0.3.1 (20.0 s vs 44.3 s over 17 pages) while converting 74% more values. On a page without prices, the extension now costs less to load than 0.3.1 did. Badges are now placed against a single page layout per pass, which halves the remaining cost on very large pages.

## Measured accuracy

Across 55 saved pages from large shops, 87% of visible prices get a visible conversion (0.3.1: 36%), as do 73% of visible measurements (0.3.1: 40%).
