EUC 1.0.2 makes the target currency easier to find and fixes the dark-mode switch.

- **Search the currency list.** The target currency menu in the popup and on the settings page has a search field. Type a code or a name, such as `IRT` or `toman`.
- **Clearer switch in dark mode.** The on/off switch now shows its thumb when on, so you can tell its state at a glance.
- **New defaults.** New installs convert to US Dollar and start in Everything mode. If you already use EUC, your saved settings don't change.
